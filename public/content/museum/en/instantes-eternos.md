"In this work, I primarily use shades of blue to convey a sense of calm, peace, and tranquility. At the same time, I seek to create a feeling of movement and fluidity through shapes and design.

The leaves featured in the artwork were not picked from trees at random; they are leaves I collect during the season when they fall naturally, respecting their own cycle and the passage of time.

For me, incorporating them into the artwork means preserving a moment of nature that would otherwise end up disappearing. Each leaf represents a stage, a moment, and a transformation: that which was once full of life falls, dries, wilts, and finds a new way to remain.

Therefore, the leaves are not simply a decorative element, but an essential part of the artwork's meaning. They represent the passage of time, transformation, and the possibility of turning something ephemeral into something that lasts.

'I speak to them and tell them that, even if time wilts them, I will make them eternal"
